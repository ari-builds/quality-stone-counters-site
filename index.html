Why the name

Eco is not a sticker on the box. It is how we build.

Most flooring gets sold on looks alone. We sell it on three other things too: what you breathe in your own home, what ends up in a landfill, and how many decades it lasts.

  • FSC-certified hardwood only. Every plank traces back to a forest that gets replanted, and we can show you the chain of custody paperwork.
  • Low-VOC, water-based finishes. No solvent smell for a week. You sleep at home the same night we finish.
  • 90% of torn-out flooring gets reclaimed or recycled. Old oak becomes new stair treads. Vinyl goes to a recycler in Charlotte, not the county dump.
  • Works in the house you have. Radiant heat, concrete slabs, 1920s subfloors. We match the system to your home instead of forcing one product on everyone.
