01 — Our story

Built the slow way,
on purpose.

We started in the Triad doing what most shops avoid: measuring the actual room, drawing the actual cabinet, and installing it with the same people who sold it. Ten years and more than 300 projects later, that is still the whole business.

We template and fabricate in-house, so the counter that arrives at your house was cut for your house. Cabinets are built to the millimetre. Installation is done by the crew that measured it.

That means we say no to some jobs. It also means the jobs we take get finished properly, on a schedule you can plan around, at the number we quoted.
