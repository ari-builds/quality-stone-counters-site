01 — The sequence

Six steps.
No mystery.

Everything below is written into your quote, including the dates.

  1. 01

    Call or send the form

    Tell us the room, roughly what you want, and when you would like it done. We book a time that suits you — not a window that suits us.

    Same day
  2. 02

    Free in-home measure

    We measure the room, photograph the constraints, and check level, plumb, access and appliance openings. This is where most surprises get caught.

    45–60 min
  3. 03

    Design and fixed quote

    You get a layout drawing, material samples and a line-item quote. The number you sign is the number you pay.

    3–5 days
  4. 04

    Deposit and fabrication

    Materials are ordered and your cabinets and stone are built in our shop. You approve the slab and the sample door before we cut.

    1–3 weeks
  5. 05

    Installation

    Our own crew sets cabinets, templates and installs the stone, then cleans up. You get a daily heads-up on what is happening next.

    1–5 days
  6. 06

    Walkthrough and warranty

    We walk the finished room with you, fix the punch list, and hand over the lifetime installation warranty.

    Same day
02 — What we need from you

Three things
that speed it up.

None of these are mandatory. All of them shorten the schedule and reduce the chance of a change order.

  1. 01

    A decision-maker

    One person who can approve the layout and the finish. Group decisions are fine — just bring them to the first meeting.

  2. 02

    Appliance choices

    Model numbers, or a decision to keep what you have. Cabinet openings depend on them.

  3. 03

    Access and parking

    Somewhere for the truck and a clear path to the room. It sounds small; it saves a day.
