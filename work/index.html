02 — Three projects, start to finish

What changed,
and why.

Greensboro · Kitchen · 6 weeks

A 1990s kitchen that could not fit a dishwasher

The room had a galley layout with a blocked corner and no room for a full-size dishwasher. We moved the range two feet, rebuilt the run as a fitted L, and set a quartz island with an overhang for stools.

  • Sage painted cabinets to the ceiling
  • Quartz island with a 12" seating overhang
  • New sink, faucet and full-height splash
Winston-Salem · Bath · 3 weeks

A bath that looked fine and leaked anyway

The tile was sound to the eye, but the shower had no membrane and the curb was flat. We stripped it back, waterproofed properly, and rebuilt with a marble slab surround and a freestanding tub.

  • Full membrane waterproofing and correct slope
  • Marble slab shower with recessed niche
  • New vanity top and hex tile floor
Winston-Salem · Stone · 9 days

A waterfall island cut from one slab

The client wanted the veining to run continuously down both ends. We laid the cut out on the slab, matched the two legs from the same block, and seamed them on the mitre so the pattern reads as one piece.

  • Book-matched waterfall ends
  • Undermount sink cut and polished in-shop
  • Seams placed outside the sightline
