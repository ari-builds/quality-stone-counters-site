02 — Compare

Which surface
suits you?

Both are excellent. They fail in different ways and suit different kitchens.

Quartz

  • Non-porous — no sealing, ever
  • Consistent colour and pattern
  • Best for busy family kitchens
  • Excellent in baths and laundry
  • Not ideal for outdoor kitchens
Quartz details

Granite

  • Natural stone, one-of-a-kind movement
  • Very heat tolerant
  • Repairable and refinishable
  • Works outdoors
  • Needs resealing every 1–3 years
Granite details
