Overview

What you are actually buying.

Most cabinets you can buy come in a handful of widths. Your kitchen did not. We build to the opening, scribe to the wall and finish the run so the last cabinet is the same size as the first.

You approve a sample door before anything is sprayed, and the drawing you sign is the drawing we build from. No substitution at install, no “close enough” filler panels.

Typical investment $250–$650 per linear foot, installed

Included

What a Quality Stone cabinet project includes.

  • 01

    Plywood boxes

    Three-quarter-inch plywood, doweled and glued — not particle board with a photo finish.

  • 02

    Real hardware

    Soft-close undermount runners and hinges rated for daily use, not builder-grade imports.

  • 03

    A sample door

    Finish, sheen and colour approved in your light before we spray the full run.

    Storage that works

    Pull-outs, dividers, appliance panels, toe-kick drawers and pantry systems planned around how you cook.

  • 05

    Scribed fit

    Cabinets fitted to your walls and ceilings, then trimmed — not shimmed and buried in caulk.

    Our own installers

    The people who measured the room are the people who set the cabinets.
